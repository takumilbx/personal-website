## Context and stakeholders

When I joined the Office of the Deputy Governor at the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration in May 2023, I had stood at both ends of an education policy without seeing the middle. With Saturday School Foundation I had taught Japanese for ten weeks at Wat Ang Keaw School in Bangkok, and in August 2019 I had shadowed a Thailand Development Research Institute researcher through fieldwork evaluating an ICT-in-education policy at a rural school. The middle, where a city decides what its policies are, was new to me.

Not everyone with a stake sat inside city hall. There was the Deputy Governor, who needed to present the city's education agenda as one picture, and the 437 school principals who would carry it into schools. There were the public and private stakeholders behind each policy, and the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities selection committee, to whom the city was putting its case. At the far end of every policy were students, teachers and parents, who live with what it does.

## The problem

Forty-seven policies is a list, not a strategy. The city's education policies existed as 47 separate items, with nothing to show which pulled in the same direction or who was responsible for each. Until they were consolidated, the Deputy Governor could not present them to principals or UNESCO as one whole.

## What I did

I read all 47 policies and consolidated them into 8 focus areas, so that every policy had a home. For each one I mapped the public and private stakeholders behind it, which turned a list into a picture of who was carrying the work. From that base I built the pitch deck and data visualisations the Deputy Governor would present, which had to work in two very different rooms: a hall of principals and an international selection committee.

## What the evidence showed

The Deputy Governor presented the deck and visualisations to 437 school principals and to the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities selection committee. That is the outcome the record supports: a pile of 47 policies became something a hall of principals could take in at once.
