Takumyi Design Directions

Three visual directions for Takumi Oshiyama's personal site, stacked so they can be compared. Each block below is styled only with its own fonts and colour tokens, so the differences are rendered rather than described. Portrait and thumbnails are solid placeholders.

Switch the theme to compare light and dark. Narrow the window to phone width to check how each direction stacks. Details, contrast figures and the recommendation are in docs/design-directions.md.

Direction A

Register

A well-kept record. One type family carries English, Thai and Japanese; numbers and metadata sit in monospace; corners are square and lists look like lists.

IBM Plex Sans · IBM Plex Sans Thai · IBM Plex Sans JP · IBM Plex Mono for metadata. Accent: teal. Radius: 0.

Direction B

Feature

A magazine feature rather than a journal article. A serif reading face at 18 px, borderless cards separated by space and a single rule, and a plum accent that appears only on buttons and underlines.

Source Serif 4 · Noto Serif Thai · Noto Serif JP for reading. Source Sans 3 · Noto Sans Thai · Noto Sans JP for buttons, tags and meta. Accent: plum. Radius: 3 px.

Direction C

Explainer

The site explains things plainly, the way the channel does: heavier headings, short lines, filled chips, and one amber button that is unmistakably the thing to press. Latin and Thai come from one Bangkok-drawn family.

Anuphan for Latin and Thai · Zen Kaku Gothic New for Japanese. Accent: amber, used as a fill and never as text. Radius: 10 to 12 px.
